Reece designed a set of amazing characters to show off the enhanced capabilities of [Rubberhose 3](https://battleaxe.co/rubberhose), and because he works out most of his shots with drawings, a lot of viewers did not believe it was actually rigged. 

![rubberhose-v3-skeleton.gif](rubberhose-v3-skeleton.gif)
I worked to match the drawn framers with basic hoses then added styling after.

![rubberhose-v3-procedural.gif](rubberhose-v3-procedural.gif)

Many of the systems that made it into the product were designed while trying to pull off these shots.
