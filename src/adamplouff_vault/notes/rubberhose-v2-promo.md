Built over the course of a year while working at the Google campus in Mountain View. My back was always sore from carrying around 2 macbooks, so during lunch, I could pick away at the code. Don't tell 🤫.

During 2016 there was a ton of amazing work created with [Rubberhose](/rubberhose). I had always dreamed of working with these artists and studios, and because of sharing my code, I finally got a chance. I can't express how humbling this felt. 

During development, I began to enjoy creating music again after selling all my gear 10 years earlier. I don't remember exactly how this was produced but I think I cut together a drum loop and some individual synth hits I recorded on my phone, then sequenced it in Premiere. Not the best workflow, but sharing sounds became a big part of why I started working on these [films](/films).
