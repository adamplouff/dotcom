Round 18 of [9 Squares](http://9-squares.tumblr.com/) was an introspective look into the past with a round one reunion. We were all so young, so innocent, so much more hair. Ah the good ol days.

It really has been great to see this project evolve into something no one could have imagined. Lots of love to @Al Boardman, [@David Stanfield](https://dribbble.com/DavidBStanfield) and [@Skip Dolphin Hursh](https://dribbble.com/skiphursh) for putting it together.

Been traveling and late to post. It's been a long time since I've made anything that wasn't RubberHose related and I really just wanted to do something dumb, halloween-y and cel.
