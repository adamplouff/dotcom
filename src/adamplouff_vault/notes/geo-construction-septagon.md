7 is a pretty crazy number that appears to have a strong significance in most religions (as well as being the defining trait of the main religion in Westeros).

The septagon (or heptagon, depending on who you ask) is one of the only regular polygons that cannot be perfectly constructed with a compass and ruler. This is simply an approximation that gets us within a degree of accuracy.
