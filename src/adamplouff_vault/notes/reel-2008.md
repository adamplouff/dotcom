Just a heads up: this is not good work. I was doing my best, but it's not good. Presented as a complete picture of progress made over the course of a career. 

I guess I had just learned about particles and awful reflective 3D.

I am grateful to have had the chance so early in my career to do composite work on such an amazing PBS rebrand for [Primal Screen](https://www.primalscreen.com/) (RIP), and to work with Alton Brown. I wasn't qualified for either but if you get a chance you rise to the occasion.

I lied my ass off to get the first job and they gave me a crash course in traditional animation. Working briefly with Alton taught me that you can (and should) work with any material you can get your hands on.
