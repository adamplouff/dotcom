Once a year, all the internet people come together to play pranks on the users. I started at Google in January and by week 2 was thrilled to be working on this project with some amazing people. Available on your phone for one day only, we built a set of 6 unique characters and dances to be found through multiple entry points.

Rigging was done with [RubberHose](http://www.battleaxe.co/rubberhose) and some [Puppet Tools](http://aescripts.com/puppettools/) for the torso.

Design lead: Judy Zhao  
Character design: by [Ruben Villa](http://www.fuchilafresheners.com/)  
Animation [Homer Rutledge](https://dribbble.com/hello_im_homer) and Adam Plouff
