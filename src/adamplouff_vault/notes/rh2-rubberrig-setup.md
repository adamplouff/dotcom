Following up on this [[rh2-rubberrig|shot]] I wanted to show a little more about how easy it is to set this up. It's a bit different way to connect two layers but it's really smooth once you get the hang of it.

Align two layers at the center of the comp (apostrophe on the keyboard to show the center cross). Select the top then the bottom layers. Press the button. It's that easy.

More about that at the [BrainDump](https://battleaxe.co/docs/rubberhose-build).
