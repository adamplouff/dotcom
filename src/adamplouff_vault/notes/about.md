My name is Adam, and I tinker for a living. My primary work right now is [battleaxe.co](https://battleaxe.co), where I build workflows for designers and animators. Before that, I worked at Google. And before that I worked in broadcast TV, creating commercials to fast-forward through. Before that, I worked at Kinkos, delivered pizzas, DJ'd, taught karate, painted faces and made balloon animals. 

My first business was as a birthday party magician. I was 12. I had business cards. It had my pager number on it. I thought it was very cool. 

This site is an attempt to preserve some of the work that I have built over the years.
