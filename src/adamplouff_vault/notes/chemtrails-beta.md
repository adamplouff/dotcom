![Chemtrails](motionographer_chemtrailsbeta.gif)

Because of a current project needing some motion trails and many changes to it's motion path, I wanted a dynamic system that would allow me to change motion without having to redraw/re-trim trails to match speed. It started off as straight lines but as we all know, arcs are more pleasing I wanted trails that could follow arcs.

There's a boatload of math going on here but with it you can:  
–select a target layer  
–pick an anchor to draw trails from  
–choose the number of trails  
–set their length and start delay  
–define a set of colors  
–then you can randomize all that mess
