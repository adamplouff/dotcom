![RubberPin](rh2-rubberpin.gif)

Love em or hate em, working with Puppet Pins has never been easier. RubberPin takes all the simplicity of RubberHose controllers and allows them to control a set of 3 pins. Stretch the rig beyond its defined length and add length to the rig itself. Change the bend direction and add auto-rotation to the end controllers.

This new rig type also aids in the grouping of pins and art to allow you to reposition, scale and rotate a whole pin group. Select 3 keys and one click.

Oh, you can also attach an art layer to a hose group, so you don't have to manually deform tattoos or complex shading. That's pretty cool too.

RubberHose 2 will be available Jan 11. That's tomorrow.
