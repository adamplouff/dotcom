Following up on this [[rh2-rubberpin|shot]] the setup for a RubberPin rig is super simple. Add three pins, select those pins, and hit the button. It works from top to bottom within your comp to determine the order to link them. It also adds a group layer to position, scale, and rotate the whole group. That right there is pretty helpful.  

More about that at the [BrainDump](https://battleaxe.co/docs/rubberhose-build).

RubberHose 2 is available now at [battleaxe.co](http://www.battleaxe.co/)
