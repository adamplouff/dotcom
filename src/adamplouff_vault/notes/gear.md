## Analog

I code on the computer, but I work with my brain. Paper helps me slow down and clearly understand the problem before trying to solve it.
### [Hobinichi Weeks Mega](https://www.atlasstationers.com/collections/hobonichi-weeks)

After trying a lot of notebooks and systems, I feel like *Weeks* nails the structure of capturing what was important in the day + the flexibility of blank pages. 
### [Leather Weeks case](https://www.atlasstationers.com/products/galen-leather-hobonichi-weeks-notebook-cover?variant=43461094670497)

I do not treat my notebooks with a lot of respect, so this keeps the cover from getting too gross, looks better over time, and has a nice pen holder that perfectly fits ↓ 

### [Pilot Hi-Tec-C Coleto 1000 4 Color Multi Pen](https://www.jetpens.com/Pilot-Hi-Tec-C-Coleto-1000-4-Color-Multi-Pen-Body-Component-Black/pd/24119)

I'm a child of the 90s and multi-color pens are a key part of my note-taking experience. I prefer the scratchy nature of Hi-Tec-C 0.3mm tips and stumbled onto this one at a [manga shop](https://usa.kinokuniya.com/) outside of Atlanta and fell in love with being able to pick my colors. 

## Hardware

### [Glorious GMMK Pro 75%](https://www.gloriousgaming.com/products/glorious-gmmk-pro-75-barebone-black)

Like many humans seeking control and customization over my domain of expertise, I fell down the mechanical keyboard rabbit hole. I tried some boards and built some boards, and found that this one hits all the criteria for me:

- **Metal** - it must feel sturdy on my desk
- **Smaller** - deskspace is precious
- **Arrow keys** - I don't want to use modifiers for navigating around
- **Fn keys** - While I don't work much in Ae, I still prefer hardware keys for functions

**Switches**: [Panda](https://www.gloriousgaming.com/products/glorious-panda-mechanical-switches). I've had *Cherry Browns* before but decided to test these out and they sound and feel great to me. 

**Caps**:  [Zambumon MT3 Serika](https://drop.com/buy/drop-zambumon-mt3-serika-custom-keycap-set). I really like bold letters in the center of the cap. None of of that tiny stuff in the corner

### [Logitech MX Master 3S](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/products/mice/mx-master-3s.910-006557.html)

Enough buttons to customize the experience, and simple enough to not think about. It's popular for a reason.

I use the ←/→ thumb buttons for back/forward whenever possible. Yes, in a browser, but also in VS Code for `Go back/forward in Edit Locations`. 

I am attempting to reduce context switching whenever possible.
